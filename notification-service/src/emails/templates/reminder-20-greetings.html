👋🏿 Master the Art of Greetings

Ẹ kú àbọ̀, {{firstName}}! (Welcome!)

In Yorùbá culture, greetings are sacred—they show respect, acknowledge elders, and establish connection before any conversation.

There are greetings for every occasion: for someone working, for someone resting, for morning, afternoon, and evening.

Mastering these greetings transforms how others perceive you—you become someone who truly understands the culture.

A proper greeting can open doors, earn respect, and make elders beam with pride at your cultural awareness.

Learn the art of proper greetings today. Log in to Zabbot and practice this essential skill.

Why This Matters

Yorùbá has more greeting forms than almost any other language. Mastering them shows deep cultural respect and understanding.
